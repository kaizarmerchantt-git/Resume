Two-Phase Refrigerant Flow Boiling Simulation

Engineered a modular custom physical component library in MATLAB Simscape for two-phase refrigerant flow boiling inside horizontal tubes, extending the foundation two-phase fluid domain. Developed an automated MATLAB pipeline interfacing with high-accuracy thermodynamic equations of state (EoS) to generate 2D normalized internal energy property grids, synthesizing over 1,390 lines of declarative Simscape code per working fluid.

Translated the Thome-Wojtan (2005) two-phase flow boiling correlation into declarative differential-algebraic equations (DAEs) covering eight distinct flow regimes. Eliminated high-magnitude numerical solver oscillations (heat transfer coefficients spiking from ~753 to ~6,665 W/m²K) by replacing discrete switching boundaries with continuous blend functions over ±5% threshold guard-bands.

PhoenixThermal // Thermochemical Energy Storage

Engineered a thermochemical energy storage concept utilizing free calcium oxide (CaO) contained in industrial lignite fly ash (a hazardous byproduct with 730 million tonnes generated annually). The reversible hydration reaction (CaO + H2O ↔ Ca(OH)2 + Heat) charges using summer curtailed renewable electricity (500–550°C), stores energy indefinitely at ambient temperatures with zero thermal loss, and discharges heat at 70–90°C directly into municipal district heating networks.

Co-developed the techno-economic business model, site feasibility licensing structure, and regional energy transition roadmap for Lusatia. Delivered executive presentation slides covering market expansion, project economics, and environmental impact during the final pitch session.
